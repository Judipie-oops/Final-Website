Where memories are made

Discover the best of our capital

Come visit one of our
exciting attractions

Columbus Zoo and Zoombezi Bay A world-class zoo with 7,000+ animals paired with Central Ohio's premier waterpark, where admission supports wildlife conservation.

CoSI (Center of Science and Industry) Award-winning science museum with 300+ hands-on exhibits, a planetarium, and live shows. Has been voted #1 in the nation six times.

Columbus Museum of Art Downtown art museum founded in 1878, showcasing American and European modern art, photography, and works by beloved local artists.

Or one of our iconic landmarks

Goodale Park Columbus's oldest park, donated in 1851, and today the heart of the Short North. Home to festivals, concerts, and community events.

Scioto Mile 145 acres of parks and trails along the Scioto River in downtown Columbus, with stunning skyline views and year-round events.

Franklin Park Conservatory and Botanical Gardens A stunning botanical garden two miles from downtown, featuring exotic plants, seasonal exhibitions, and the world's only permanent Dale Chihuly glass collection in a botanical garden.

Get some good grub too!

The Pearl A lively Short North gastropub known for seasonal seafood, made-from-scratch comfort food, craft cocktails, and one of Columbus's best happy hours.

Lindey's An upscale American bistro in historic German Village with a New York energy, white tablecloths, and a nationally recognized outdoor patio.

Speck Italian eatery in downtown Columbus serving house-made pasta, seasonal dishes, and craft cocktails in a vibrant, eclectic setting.
